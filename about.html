Our Story

A Journey of Trust & Excellence

Jonathon Crisp established Henson Crisp Ltd in 2007 while anticipating the arrival of his youngest son. The firm began with 25 families and £15 million in assets under management, and now serves clients from Peterborough and Cambridge across the UK.

The firm invests in the knowledge and development of its team, and helps clients stay informed with a quarterly newsletter featuring articles and market updates.
